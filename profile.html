Voluntary Donation & Anti-Scam Policy

WUB BloodCare strictly prohibits any commercial transactions, cash payments, bKash/Nagad transfers, or blood selling. Violators will face immediate account suspension and referral to university disciplinary authorities. All blood compatibility checks and medical procedures must be conducted by certified healthcare professionals.
